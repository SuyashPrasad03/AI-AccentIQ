# Phonetics Knowledge Base — Sources

## Primary References

1. **Roach, P. (2009).** *English Phonetics and Phonology* (4th ed.). Cambridge University Press.
   - Phoneme descriptions, articulation positions, minimal pairs

2. **Ladefoged, P. & Johnson, K. (2014).** *A Course in Phonetics* (7th ed.). Cengage.
   - IPA articulation details, manner/place of articulation

3. **Swan, M. & Smith, B. (2001).** *Learner English* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
   - L1 transfer patterns, common substitutions by language background

4. **Celce-Murcia, M., Brinton, D., & Goodwin, J. (2010).** *Teaching Pronunciation* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
   - Minimal pairs, practice sentences, teaching methodology

5. **International Phonetic Association (1999).** *Handbook of the International Phonetic Association.* Cambridge University Press.
   - IPA chart, phoneme classifications

## Online Resources

- UCLA Phonetics Lab: http://www.phonetics.ucla.edu/
- IPA Chart Interactive: https://www.ipachart.com/
- English Pronunciation in Use (Cambridge): minimal pair lists

## Data Curation Notes

- All articulation descriptions are paraphrased from the above sources
- Minimal pairs selected for pedagogical value (high frequency words)
- Common errors documented from the applied linguistics literature
- Practice sentences designed for natural context + target phoneme repetition
- No copyrighted material reproduced verbatim (all descriptions rewritten)
